AI automation

Mapping 25 controls to 235 requirements

A local Ollama model shortlisted requirement matches for each control, and all 139 suggestions were reviewed by a named person before anything reached the gap analysis. Two Trust Scores are reported because 15 reference rows were added during the review, 12 of them pairs the model itself had suggested.

Where the model is reliable, and where it is not trust against recall, per framework

A framework with high trust and low recall is one the model is quiet about rather than wrong about. COBIT and OT are the two that carry mandatory human review.
